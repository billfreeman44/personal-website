Open data

Public PureSkill.gg Data

Competitive Counter-Strike (CS2) match data from matchmaking, FACEIT, and other third-parties. Contains full player telemetry and timestamped game events. These data are extracted from CS2/CS:GO replay files called demos. Data for each match is split across 33 files, collectively called csds. These data are available publicly for research, independent learning, or your own curiosity.
