Overwatch Loot Box Simulation

A Monte Carlo simulation analysis of the loot box system in Overwatch.

Preface

These pages are a project that was a demonstration of using random numbers, also known as Monte Carlo simulation, to do analysis. The subject of study is the loot box system in the game Overwatch. I wrote this up as a simple project to get into data science. It is also comprehensive, so it is rather long.

If you already know what Overwatch and loot boxes are you can skip to Part 2: Probabilities and Simulation.

If you want to skip the report and simulate opening loot boxes for yourself, then go to the Loot Box Simulator.

I am not affiliated with Blizzard in any way. Use the information here at your own risk. The numbers on these pages were for the items as of Apr 24, 2017. There are more items now.
